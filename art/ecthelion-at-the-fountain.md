# Ecthelion at the King’s Fountain

[First illustration](ecthelion-at-the-fountain-study-01.png)

In the early *Fall of Gondolin*, Tuor is down and Ecthelion has lost the use of his shield-arm. A second wound costs him his sword. When Gothmog raises his whip, Ecthelion leaps. Both perish in the deep basin. The painting takes the instant he leaves his sword behind. His face, caught in the light off the water, is where the viewer can see what the leap costs him. [*The Fall of Gondolin*, “The Original Tale”](https://www.ebookscape.com/middle-earth/the-fall-of-gondolin-j-r-r-tolkien/)

The silver armour and the diamond point of the helm draw on the later account of Tuor meeting Ecthelion at the gate. Gothmog’s shape in the smoke and the carving on the fountain are the illustrator’s choices. [*Unfinished Tales*, “Of Tuor and His Coming to Gondolin”](https://afrodita.rcub.bg.ac.rs/~alexp/sf/tolkien/gond-fall.html)
